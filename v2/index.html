Put it on your home screen
iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: browser menu, "Add to Home screen". A home-screen icon keeps its own separate storage, so use the app in the same place (same icon, or same browser) to keep one list.
What stays where
Tasks, streams, photos and settings are stored only in this browser on this device (IndexedDB). Nothing is sent anywhere - the app makes no network requests. Clearing browser data or removing the home-screen app erases it, so keep a backup if it matters.
