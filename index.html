Put it on your home screen
iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". It then opens full-screen like an app and keeps its own storage. Android: browser menu, "Add to Home screen".
What stays where
Tasks, streams, photos and settings are stored only in this browser on this device (IndexedDB). Nothing is sent anywhere - the app makes no network requests. Exporting downloads a file to your device; restoring reads a file you choose. Clearing browser data or removing the home-screen app erases it, so keep a backup if it matters.
